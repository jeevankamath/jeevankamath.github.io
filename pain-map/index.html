Before you start

This is not a diagnosis. Pain Map is an educational prototype. It suggests possible areas for sports-related pain, based on published physiotherapy research. It cannot examine you and may be wrong. It does not replace an assessment by a qualified doctor or physiotherapist.

Emergency: call 112 now if you have chest pain, numbness or weakness in both legs or around your genitals or bottom, new loss of bladder or bowel control, pain after a serious fall or accident, or you feel very unwell.
