How I work

Frameworks I actually use

Ask → Dig → Insight

A call method built on six discovery questions:

  1. Door: how you got in
  2. Pain: what's actually hurting
  3. Number: what it costs
  4. Scope / stack: what's in place today
  5. Why now: what's changed
  6. People: who else cares

14-day multichannel sequence

Calls, email and social, spaced deliberately over two weeks so every touch has a reason.

Speed-to-lead rule

When someone raises a hand, the clock starts. A simple rule for responding while intent is warm.

20-module onboarding kit

Four phases. Each module has one page, one short video, a one-screen SOP and a self-check.
